# Zruxr 1.2.6

- **Uninstall like Steam.** Uninstall deletes a game's files but keeps it in your library. Install it again anytime.
- **Download stats.** Speed graph, time left, average speed and finish time for every download.
- **Friends.** Add friends by username and see who is playing what. You choose what to share.
- **Downloads sections.** Browse by Big titles, Co-op, Horror, Open world and more, like a store.
- **Tray.** Closing the window now keeps Zruxr running in the tray, so your games keep being tracked. Quit from the tray icon, or turn it off in Settings.
- **Create-account screen** only shows the first time after installing.
- **Themes.** Pick Dark, Midnight, Violet, Crimson, Forest or Light in Settings.
- **Antivirus warning.** If a download vanishes after saving, Zruxr tells you it was probably removed by your antivirus and links to Windows Security.
- **GTA VI banner** in the Library (shows once you've added a source).
- **Safer:** sign-in lockout after repeated wrong passwords, crash-proof saving, one copy of Zruxr at a time, tighter page security.
