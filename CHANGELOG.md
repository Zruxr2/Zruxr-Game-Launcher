# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Scan PC: find installed games from Steam, Epic, GOG and your Games folders, or scan a single folder or the whole PC.
- Discover row on the home page: top sellers, top free, new releases and specials from Steam.
- Optional accounts: sign in with a username to sync your game list and total play time between PCs.
- Stats tab with total hours, this week, a 7-day chart and most played games.
- Automatic cover art with a generated fallback, re-checked weekly.
- Sort options, favorites, search shortcut and cover glow.
- Settings: cover size, start with Windows, startup animation, Discover toggle.
